# GradTrack Graduate/Alumni Presentation — Voice-over Script

Narrator: professional English, `en-PH-RosaNeural`, calm and conversational. All names, contact details, posts, messages, announcements, and jobs shown in the presentation are fictional sample data.

## 00:00–00:10 — Introduction

Welcome to GradTrack, a web-based graduate tracer system with alumni job support for Norzagaray College.

## 00:10–00:21 — Graduate verification

Graduates begin by verifying their identity using information that matches the institution's official graduate records.

## 00:21–00:45 — Graduate Tracer Survey

After verification, graduates review the privacy notice and complete the Graduate Tracer Survey. The updated form organizes personal information, educational background, employment data, and career experience into clear sections, saves progress as a draft, and provides a final review before submission.

## 00:45–01:02 — Survey completion and account creation

After submitting the survey, eligible graduates can review their verified details and create a Graduate Portal account. Passwords remain masked, and new accounts are submitted for Alumni Admin verification.

## 01:02–01:13 — Graduate Portal login

Once approved, graduates can log in securely. The updated portal opens on the Community Forum as the main graduate view.

## 01:13–01:32 — Community Forum

The Community Forum allows graduates to read alumni posts, react, comment, and share useful career experiences. Graduates can create their own posts and manage the content they have submitted while following the community guidelines.

## 01:32–01:44 — Announcements

Announcements keep graduates informed about alumni activities, career programs, college updates, and other relevant opportunities.

## 01:44–02:03 — Direct messages and group chats

The unified Messages area supports both direct and group conversations. Graduates can communicate privately with fellow alumni, or participate in group discussions for career coordination, batch activities, and community events.

## 02:03–02:30 — Job opportunities and job posting

Browse Jobs presents approved opportunities with searchable details such as position, company, location, program fit, requirements, and application information. Eligible employed graduates can also submit sample job openings for Alumni Admin review. GradTrack supports access to opportunities, but does not guarantee employment.

## 02:30–02:48 — Graduate profile and settings

Graduates can review their profile, contact information, education, and current employment details. Editable profile settings help alumni keep career information current without changing the tracer survey they already submitted.

## 02:48–03:00 — Notifications

Notifications bring new jobs, forum activity, and announcements together, helping graduates quickly return to important updates.

## 03:00–03:16 — Closing

With GradTrack, Norzagaray College can maintain stronger connections with its graduates while supporting graduate tracing, alumni communication, community engagement, and access to career opportunities.
